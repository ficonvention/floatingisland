About

Seoul, the world's representative MICE Unique Venue

Located at the end of Banpo Bridge beside the Rainbow Fountain, Some Sevit Floating Island was built as part of the Han River Renaissance Project to establish a world-class landmark for cultural events on the Han River. The unique artificial-island platform and the LED lighting on the outer walls create a stunning view of the river.

Gabit Island — the first island, where Floating Island Convention is located — is a design building that expresses a flower in full bloom: a venue where international conferences, exhibitions, performances and ceremonies are held for guests from home and abroad.

See & Do

Things to do

Yacht Tour

Ride a yacht and enjoy the night view of the Han River with the cool river breeze. Yachts are available at various times — from bright daylight to sunset to colorful night views.

Rainbow Fountain

Across the full 1,140 m span of Banpo Bridge, water is sprayed to music so that fountain and soundtrack blend in harmony. At night, landscape lighting turns it into a fantastic river nightscape.

Beer Garden

Enjoy the night view of Sebit Island with hand-crafted beer, wine and a variety of food at the Sebit Island outdoor garden with an open view of the Han River.
